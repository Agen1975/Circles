Cosmic Bubbles

Complete the circles

Get it on Google Play

Every piece is one quarter of a glowing bubble. Move pieces anywhere on the 8×8 board and fit four of the same colour together to pop a complete circle. Chain moves to keep the board clear, link circles into clusters, and build a streak of up to ×5.

  • Classic: endless play until the board fills up
  • Daily puzzle: the same board for everyone, 30 moves
  • Journey: 60 levels with goals and stars
  • Blitz: as many points as you can in 60 seconds
  • Zen: no game over, just relax

Leaderboards for Classic, Blitz and the Daily puzzle are on Google Play Games.
