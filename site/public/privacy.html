Read the full privacy policy

In short

  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings → Privacy in the game.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) help us fix bugs and improve the game.
  • If you use Google Play Games, your scores are submitted to its leaderboards under your Play Games profile.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
